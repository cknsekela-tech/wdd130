Vortex Rapids Co.

At Vortex Rapids Co., we don't just guide river trips—we unleash adrenaline. For over a decade, our expert team has been conquering the wildest Class V rapids, offering thrill-seekers an unmatched, heart-pounding white water experience. If you're ready to test your limits and ride the vortex, grab a paddle.

History

Founded in 2015 by a group of renegade river guides, Vortex Rapids Co. started with just two cracked rafts and a passion for roaring water. Over the years, we have mapped out uncharted river bends, rescued wandering kayakers, and grown into the region's premier extreme outfitters. Today, we host thousands of adventurers a year, but our core mission has never changed: providing safe, unforgettable, and absolutely wild river runs.
